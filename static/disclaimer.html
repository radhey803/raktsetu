Clinical & Source Attribution Disclaimer

Read before using BloodGrid for medical decisions

1. Not an Official Government Portal

BloodGrid is an independent open data lakehouse initiative. We are not owned, operated, or endorsed by the National Health Mission (NHM), National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC), National Informatics Centre (NIC), or the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW).

2. Mandatory Phone Confirmation Before Travelling

Blood inventories in hospital blood centres change dynamically due to emergency surgeries, trauma transfusions, and clinical reservations. While our lakehouse synchronizes data on a scheduled basis, reporting lag exists between hospital physical dispensing and portal entry.

NEVER TRAVEL TO A BLOOD BANK WITHOUT FIRST CALLING THE FACILITY ON THE TELEPHONE TO CONFIRM UNIT RESERVATION AND REQUISITION REQUIREMENTS.

3. Emergency Contact Numbers

For life-critical emergencies, dial India's national helplines immediately:

  • Emergency Services: 112
  • National Health Helpline: 104
  • Ambulance Services: 108 / 102
