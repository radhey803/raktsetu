1. Nature of the Service

BloodGrid is an independent data intelligence and blood availability locator platform operated by BloodGrid Technologies. BloodGrid is NOT an official government portal, healthcare provider, or emergency medical service.

All availability records are aggregated from public registers published on eRaktKosh (Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India). We make no warranty that reported stock will be reserved upon arrival. Users and clinical teams must call the blood centre by telephone to confirm reservation before travelling.

2. Acceptable Use & Restrictions

  • You may query availability for emergency patient care, family coordination, and non-profit transfusion planning.
  • Resale Prohibited: You may NOT resell raw data extracted from BloodGrid or claim proprietary ownership over eRaktKosh public records.
  • No Impersonation: You must never claim to be an official representative or liaison of the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare or eRaktKosh.
  • Automated Abuse: Scraping the web front-end is prohibited. Commercial and automated consumers must use official developer API keys subject to tier rate limits.

3. Subscriptions, Fees & Cancellation

Paid tiers (Pro Alerts, Developer API, NGO Team) are billed on a monthly recurring basis in Indian Rupees (₹) via our payment gateway (Razorpay). Prices exclude applicable 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST SAC 998313).

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account console. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. In the event of a failed renewal, an automated 3-day grace period is provided prior to account downgrading.
