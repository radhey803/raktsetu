Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Data Fiduciary: BloodGrid Technologies

1. Principle of Purpose Limitation & Data Minimization

We collect only the bare minimum personal data required to deliver real-time stock notifications and developer services:

  • Public Search: Zero personal data or cookies required. All searches are anonymous.
  • Alert Subscriptions: Recipient Email or Telegram Chat ID, requested Blood Group, and target District.
  • Commercial Billing: Billing entity name, email address, and optional GSTIN. Payment card details are handled entirely by Razorpay PCI-DSS certified infrastructure and never touch our servers.

2. Prohibition on Donor Health Data

BloodGrid does NOT collect, store, or process individual donor medical records, blood donation eligibility records, or patient transfusion health histories in this release. All inventory records represent facility-level aggregated units.

3. Rights of the Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you hold the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Every alert dispatch contains a 1-click cryptographic unsubscribe link.
  • Right of Erasure: You may request complete erasure of your account, API keys, and notification subscriptions by contacting privacy@bloodgrid.in. Requests are processed within 48 hours.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Inquiries may be directed to our designated Data Protection Officer at dpo@bloodgrid.in.

4. Retention Period

Alert subscriptions are retained only while active. Inactive or unsubscribed email records are automatically purged from our operational stores after 30 days.
